Newsletter: May 6th, 2025
MIT IEEE/ACM is MIT's student branch of the International Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers and the Association of Computing Machinery.

Our organization aims to create and support a close community between students and faculty within the EECS department.

Stay updated on all our events through this biweekly newsletter!

Upcoming Events
In this edition of our newsletter, we have some exciting events planned that we’d love to share with you!

Coding and Cake Study Break

MIT IEEE/ACM is inviting you to a Coding and Cake Study Break on Tuesday, May 6th at 7:30 PM in Room 4-231. Come take a break from studying to enjoy delicious cake from Flour, play a fun round of IEEE/ACM-themed bingo, and get the chance to win free merch! It's a great opportunity to relax and connect with other Course 6 students in a casual and welcoming environment. We hope to see you there!
 

Site Visit + Poster Session @ Microsoft NERD Office

MIT IEEE/ACM is excited to invite you to a Site Visit and Poster Session at the Microsoft NERD Office on Tuesday, May 13th from 3–5 PM at 1 Memorial Drive, Cambridge, MA 02143.

Join us for a science fair–style event where teams from Microsoft will be sharing their ongoing projects in an interactive, poster-session format. You’ll have the opportunity to explore the work that excites you most, connect with Microsoft engineers and researchers, and engage in informal networking with professionals across the company.

Light snacks will be provided, and Microsoft will also be opening up their makerspace for students to explore. Please RSVP here by Sunday, May 11th!

 

Matcha & Major Declaration

MIT IEEE/ACM is inviting you to a relaxing afternoon of matcha and mentorship at our Matcha & Major Declaration event on Saturday, May 11th at 4 PM in 32-144. Whether you're deciding between 6-5, 6-3, 6-7, or 6-4—or just want to learn more about what each major has to offer—come chat with upperclassmen, get your questions answered, and enjoy some delicious matcha-themed treats. It’s a great chance to connect with Course 6 peers and ease any stress around the major declaration process. We hope to see you there!
 

Sushi Social

MIT IEEE/ACM is excited to announce our Sushi Social on Wednesday, May 14th from 7–9 PM in Room 4-149! Take a break from studying and recharge with fresh sushi, good company, and a relaxing atmosphere. It’s the perfect way to destress and enjoy some delicious food before finals. We hope to see you there!
 

Course 6/EECS Merch

Get your Course 6 hoodies ($25) and stickers ($2.50) here — order by Sunday, May 11th at 11:59 PM!

Feeling the finals crunch? Treat yourself to some Course 6 merch and rep EECS in style!

We’re offering cozy Course 6 hoodies in two color options—dark green and navy blue—as well as three unique die-cut laptop sticker designs. You can check out all the designs in detail at the link above.

Don’t miss out—quantities are limited, and we can’t guarantee extras. Happy shopping and good luck with finals!

Recent Events

MBG x IEEE/ACM Mixer

MIT IEEE/ACM was excited to co-host a special mixer with the MIT Biotech Group on Tuesday, April 29th, as part of Biotech Immersion Week. The evening featured an engaging AI panel that brought together faculty working at the cutting edge of biology and computation, offering students a glimpse into the rapidly evolving intersection of technology and the life sciences. Attendees enjoyed a delicious dinner, made new connections, and had the chance to explore innovative research in an interdisciplinary setting. Thank you to everyone who joined us for this amazing event!
