Newsletter: October 14th, 2025
MIT IEEE/ACM is MIT's student branch of the International Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers and the Association of Computing Machinery.

Our organization aims to create and support a close community between students and faculty within the EECS department.

Welcome to those who recently signed up for our mailing list! Stay updated on all our events through this biweekly newsletter.
 

Upcoming Events

Be on the lookout for email blasts in the next couple weeks with information about a spooky-themed study break for the end of October and a faculty dinner with Professor Andreea Bobu on October 29th! 

September Events Recap

Career Week Events

Leading up to the fall career fair, MIT IEEE/ACM was excited to host a series of events connecting students with recruiters and engineers from technology companies, including Q&As, product demonstrations, and information sessions.

 

Arm Info Session + Q&A

To kick off career week, MIT IEEE/ACM hosted an informational and networking session with Arm, a leading semiconductor and software design company in the space of energy-efficient compute technologies. At the event, students got to hear from Arm’s hiring managers about what working at Arm was like! They also had the opportunity to ask questions and network with Arm engineers and recruiters, including getting tips for the application cycle.

 

Persona Info Session + Q&A

Next, we hosted an event with Persona, a leading identity and security company offering various tools to businesses. Students at this tech talk/networking session heard about Persona’s products and culture firsthand from engineers and recruiters. In addition, students had the opportunity to directly engage with Persona representatives, ask questions, network, and learn about Persona’s internship and new graduate opportunities. 

 

Glean Live Product Demo + Q&A

We ended career week with a live product demo, tech talk, and Q&A session with lead engineers and hiring managers from Glean, which offers a prominent enterprise productivity platform. In addition to seeing Glean’s AI-powered platform in action and learning about its technology infrastructure, students got to hear from a panel of engineers and recruiters about Glean’s technology, culture, and opportunities for internships and new graduates. 

 

Faculty Dinner with Prof. Henry Corrigan-Gibbs

In our first faculty event of the semester, students had the opportunity to have dinner with Prof. Henry Corrigan-Gibbs, an associate professor in the EECS department at MIT who works with students in the Parallel & Distributed Operating Systems Group and Computer Security System Group in CSAIL. Before his time at MIT, Prof. Corrigan-Gibbs received a B.S. in computer science from Yale University, completed his PhD at Stanford University, and was a postdoc at EPFL in Lausanne, Switzerland. At the event, students heard about Prof. Corrigan-Gibbs’ groundbreaking research in computer security and privacy, specifically in using cryptography to create systems that are beneficial to the user and impactful in real-world deployment. Students also got a unique perspective on the broader influence of research, learning about how Prof. Corrigan-Gibbs’ research has influenced NIST standards and how his systems have been used at companies including Apple, Google, and Mozilla.
